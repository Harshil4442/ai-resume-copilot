## Implemented behavior

A distinct version-four review authorizes one Greenhouse **Submit application** click. Older fill and attachment reviews do not acquire submit authority. The review binds the candidate, saved application, company, numeric opening and exact HTTPS destination, every supported current answer, original/custom/tailored selection and sealed artifact metadata, current Chrome document/form context, completed-upload continuity and the 20-credit policy. Unsupported or incomplete controls, login and visible CAPTCHA stop for candidate handling and another review. No citizenship, work authorization or employment is inferred.

Attachment handling now supports the publicly observed visually hidden Resume/CV input and its upload/unmount transition. The extension verifies the selected File against the approved bytes before callbacks. A private isolated-world monitor retains only the approved selection identity/hash and watches the known upload subtree through completion. Removing/replacing the file, including uploading different bytes under the same filename in the same document, invalidates continuity. A reload or missing monitor also stops final submission. Durable minimal selection provenance survives answer-payload expiry; it does not become a provider receipt.

Final approval reserves exactly 20 prepaid application credits through the existing credit policy and admission ledger. A durable possible-send attempt and one-shot begin receipt are committed before native I/O. The independent native boundary freshly checks protected subject/device ownership, current execution epoch, publication resources and immutable opening-level consumption. It retains a full journaled submit intent and consumes an original opaque one-use export acknowledgement. There is no SQL/cached-epoch fallback. The service then reacquires SQL locks and rechecks flags, cancellation, expiry, credentials, source/opening, artifact generation/package and export state before returning the original ten-second lease. It never extends a deadline or reconstructs a lost acknowledgement.

The browser validates the exact current form and completed upload again, then clicks only the known native submit button once. Greenhouse owns its validation, CAPTCHA and fetch handler. The companion constructs no raw employer POST and has no submit retry loop. Lost begin/outcome replies, stale leases, altered approvals and unknown callbacks stop. Cancellation/expiry before a possible-send claim refunds the reservation and releases the opening; after a claim the hold and safety budget remain for reconciliation. Late browser observations/cancellation cannot overwrite an independently reconciled confirmed or verified-not-submitted application.

Read-only reconciliation can capture a confirmation view, validation error, candidate checkpoint or uncertainty. These are explicitly device observations. They cannot create an employer receipt, settle credits or infer rejection. Existing independent administrator reconciliation still requires the employer's accepted complete receipt contract/proof; final browser status respects its terminal result.
