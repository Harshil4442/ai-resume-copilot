# HireWiz blinded quality review rubric

This is a draft protocol for two independent human reviewers. It is not an approved reference baseline. The coordinator must approve and freeze the inputs, supported cohorts, reference outputs, rubric, statistical design and budget before collecting held-out results. Synthetic fixture truth labels do not represent real candidate approval.

Review the provided source resume, job and approved fixture evidence before judging each labelled output. Review A and B independently. Do not try to infer which is the existing route. Do not consult the coordinator mapping, model names, cost or another reviewer's judgments. If identity information appears in the packet, stop and notify the coordinator.

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Use the same anchors for both labels. Add a concrete reason; do not enter a score if evidence is missing.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Relevance | Addresses a different goal | Mostly generic or misses key requirements | Addresses the role with material omissions | Specific to the selected role and evidence | Consistently precise and prioritizes the most relevant requirements |
| Completeness | Missing or unusable deliverable | Several required parts missing | Core result present with a material omission | All required parts present | Complete and well integrated without unnecessary content |
| Clarity | Unclear or contradictory | Difficult to follow | Understandable with some ambiguity | Clear and well organized | Clear, precise and easy to act on |
| Usefulness | Cannot support the requested task | Requires major correction | Useful after material revision | Useful with minor review | Directly useful after normal candidate review |

For a paid interview set, count eight distinct role-relevant questions and actionable guidance. Missing personal evidence must be marked; invented first-person answers fail. For matching, verify hard eligibility against the source and distinguish missing evidence from lack of capability. Scores must not be described as hiring probabilities.

For changed personal claims, verify every name, employer, date, qualification, tool and metric against approved evidence. A citation ID alone does not prove support. Check responsibility, causal claims and strengthened verbs, not only numbers. For learning plans, confirm concrete future steps address the selected gaps and references have a current review. Suggested projects must never appear as completed achievements.

For tailoring, inspect both native structure and every rendered page. Check section order, type treatment, geometry, links, unaffected content, reading order, clipping, overlap, blank pages and missing content. The reviewed artifact hash must match the downloadable artifact. A different template or unchanged source snapshot is not a successful source-preserving edit. Unsupported format/layout must be declined explicitly without a successful-delivery charge.

Mark each critical defect field `true` or `false`: factual_error, privacy_error and document_corruption. Any positive flag blocks the route pending independent resolution. Do not average a critical defect away using a high total score. A missing artifact, missing source evidence or missing page review is incomplete evidence, not a passing result.

Two distinct reviewers must score every output. An independent third person adjudicates every score disagreement using the source and both written reasons. Record the final score and explanation. Reviewers and adjudicator must use their actual authorized identities in the evidence packet; placeholders are not approval.

The analyst uses only the prespecified held-out set and computes candidate minus approved reference for each paired metric within every important cohort. An appropriately powered, independently reviewed method must supply the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound with zero tolerated degradation. The packet tool checks bindings, counts, score arithmetic and reported bounds; it does not compute or certify the confidence interval or power. Inconclusive results require more evidence or retention of the funded approved reference route.

AI grading may assist triage but cannot fill the human-review columns or act as release authority. Freeze model, prompt, validator and scope versions with the results. Hold wider rollout until independent quality and financial review approves the exact route and evidence digests.
